Mouse

Keyboard

TabMoves through the controls; the 3D view and the sequence are one stop each
Arrow keysPin the next or previous residue; in the sequence, Up and Down move ten
FComes to the pinned residue
RResets the view
EscCloses a dialog, leaves Measure or Labels, lets go of the pin, or leaves the Advanced view, in that order
